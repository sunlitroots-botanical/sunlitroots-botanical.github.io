What this site does not do

It has no accounts, no checkout, no payment form, no analytics, no advertising trackers, no cookies, and no third-party scripts or fonts.

Your basket

If you add things to the basket, the list is saved only in your own browser’s local storage on your device. It is not sent to us. You can clear it by removing the items or clearing your browser’s site data.

When you write us

The Write us form opens your own email app. What you send arrives in our inbox like any email, and we use it only to answer you. Please do not send card numbers or payment details.
