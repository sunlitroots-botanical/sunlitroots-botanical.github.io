The house

Sunlit Roots, from Heartland Homestead.

The name is the brief: sun on the worktable, roots in fat and herb. Sunlit Roots Botanical makes soap, beeswax-and-shea-wax balms, soy and coconut-apricot candles, wax melts, and a small dog line at Heartland Homestead — slowly, and without pretending to be a factory.

Tallow is not a trend here. It is the old way to make a bar that actually lathers in well water and a balm that does not vanish in January wind. Soy and coconut-apricot candles follow; beeswax and shea wax stay in the balm base. The dog shelf exists because the human bar is the wrong object for a coat, and because Illinois winters are rude to pads.

The look is boho rustic country because that is the room: linen, weathered wood, sage in a jar, terracotta on the sill. Marketing that tells the truth tends to look like the workbench.

The Reiki shelf — Still Room — is frankincense and lavender for the wash and the hands, and a chakra candle line for the table: seven scents in 7 oz jars, 2 oz melts, and tea lights, named for a sitting. Soap, cosmetic, and candle. The sitting is yours. The tin does not do the work.

The Faith shelf — Scripture Garden — is Magi Bar, Hyssop Wash, Rose of Sharon, Bethany Balm, a Magi candle, and a Lampstand candle. Named from the page. Soap, cosmetic, and candle. The prayer is yours.
