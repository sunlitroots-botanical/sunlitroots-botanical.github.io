What sits in the still room

  • The Still Room line is crafted by a Reiki master.
  • Seven chakra sittings in 7 oz jars and tea lights, plus a rainbow tin of all seven. The pours are scent choices on Meditation Melts, not separate boxes.
  • Meditation Melts are one 2 oz box. Choose a chakra pour, white tea and sage, or teakwood and mahogany.
  • Chakra jars keep a pinch of color flakes in the box, not on the candle wax. Meditation Melts carry the flakes on the cubes. They are not a treatment.
  • Palm Rest Balm softens dry skin. It does not recover, treat, or carry a session.
  • Peace Balm is tallow and jojoba with lavender and Roman chamomile. It softens dry skin.
  • The samplers are gift boxes. They are not kits for practice.

How we name them

  • We print the seven chakra names. They are the scent names.
  • Chakra themes describe personal intentions, not promised effects.
  • We will not print that a candle heals, treats, or moves energy.
  • A candle is a candle. The sitting — healing or otherwise — is yours.
